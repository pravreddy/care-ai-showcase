## Document Signing

### How to Sign a Document

**If you received a signing request:**

1. Check your email for "Please sign this document"
2. Click the link in the email
3. Review the document
4. Click on the signature field
5. Draw, type, or upload your signature
6. Click **"Complete Signing"**

Done! You'll receive a copy of the signed document.

### How to Send a Document for Signing

1. Click **"Documents"** → **"Upload"**
2. Upload your PDF
3. Click **"Add Signature"** where you need signatures
4. Enter signer email addresses
5. Click **"Send"**

Signers will receive an email with instructions.

# Part B: For Catering Staff

## Daily Sales Entry

If you work for a catering company, you can record daily deliveries:

### Quick Entry (Recommended)

1. Login to SignSimple
2. Click **"Sales"** in the sidebar
3. Select **Client** (company you delivered to)
4. Select **Date**
5. Enter:

| Field | What to Enter | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| Num People | Headcount served | 48 |
| Items | What you delivered | "10 Roti, 5 Rice, 5 Dal" |
| Item Price | Price per person | ₹230 |
| Packing | Packing charges | ₹100 |
| Courier | Delivery charges | ₹200 |

6. Click **"Save"**

**Total is calculated automatically!**

### Marking a Holiday

If no delivery was made:

1. Select the date
2. Check **"Is Holiday"**
3. Enter reason: "Sunday" / "Diwali" / "Company Closed"
4. Save

Holiday entries show ₹0.

### Viewing Past Entries

1. Click **"Sales"**
2. Use filters: Client, Date Range
3. See all past entries with totals

## Generating Invoices

### Create Monthly Invoice

1. Click **"Invoices"** in sidebar
2. Click **"Generate Invoice"**
3. Select:
   - Client: "TechCorp Office"
   - From: "2026-03-01"
   - To: "2026-03-31"
   - Tax %: 18 (GST)
4. Click **"Generate"**

### What You Get

- Invoice number auto-generated
- All working days summed up
- Holidays excluded
- Tax calculated
- PDF ready to download

### Sending to Client

1. Download the PDF
2. Email to client
3. Or click **"Send Invoice"** if email is configured

## Raising a Dispute

### When to Raise a Dispute

- Item was missing from delivery
- Wrong item delivered
- Food quality issue
- Quantity was less than expected
- You were overcharged

### How to Raise a Dispute

1. Go to **"My Deliveries"**
2. Find the date with the issue
3. Click **"Raise Dispute"**
4. Fill in:

| Field | What to Enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Type | Select: Missing Item / Quality Issue / etc. |
| Description | Explain the problem |
| Photo | Upload evidence (optional) |

5. Click **"Submit"**

### What Happens Next

1. Your caterer receives notification
2. They investigate
3. You'll see status updates:
   - **Open** — Submitted, waiting for response
   - **Acknowledged** — They're looking into it
   - **Resolved** — Issue fixed (credit applied / refund / etc.)
   - **Rejected** — Dispute not accepted (with reason)

### Viewing Your Disputes

1. Click **"My Disputes"**
2. See all disputes you've raised
3. Check status and resolution notes

## FAQ

### General

**Q: Is this legally binding?**
A: Yes. Electronic signatures are valid under UK law (Electronic Communications Act 2000) and Indian law (IT Act 2000).

**Q: How long are documents stored?**
A: 90 days on free plan, longer on paid plans.

### For Clients

**Q: I didn't receive my login details**
A: Contact your caterer. They create your account.

**Q: I can't see all my deliveries**
A: You can only see deliveries from when your account was linked. Ask your caterer for older records.

**Q: My dispute was rejected unfairly**
A: Contact your caterer directly to discuss.

### For Catering Staff

**Q: How do I add a new client?**
A: Ask your admin. Only admins can add clients.

**Q: I made a mistake in yesterday's entry**
A: You can edit recent entries. Go to Sales → find the entry → edit.

**Q: Client is asking for old records**
A: Go to Invoices → regenerate for the date range needed.
